# Awesome-Smart-Building-Platform

## Top Smart Building Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Building Management Systems (BMS), Energy Optimization, IoT Integration, Occupancy Analytics & Facilities Operations*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Smart Building** solutions. These platforms help facility managers and owners monitor, control, and optimize HVAC, lighting, energy, space utilization, and other building systems through IoT, analytics, and automation.



**Examples** include Facilio, BuildingMinds, Switch Automation, Spacewell, BrainBox AI, Enertiv, Honeywell Forge, Johnson Controls OpenBlue, Siemens Building X, and Schneider EcoStruxure Building (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full commercial BMS and enterprise smart-building suites remain dominant due to integration depth, certification, and long asset lifecycles. Strong open-source options exist for energy management (**MyEMS**), IoT platforms, BACnet/Modbus gateways, and home/building automation stacks. This section lists every major relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Facilio](https://facilio.com/)**  

  Cloud-native connected building platform focused on maintenance, energy, and operations workflows for commercial facilities.



- **[BuildingMinds](https://buildingminds.com/)**  

  Digital twin and portfolio platform for real-estate and building performance management.



- **[Switch Automation](https://www.switchautomation.com/)**  

  Smart building operating system that aggregates data from multiple systems for analytics, control, and optimization.



- **[Spacewell](https://spacewell.com/)**  

  Workplace and building management solutions covering space, energy, and facilities operations.



- **[BrainBox AI](https://brainboxai.com/)**  

  AI-driven HVAC optimization platform that autonomously improves comfort and energy efficiency (now associated with larger HVAC ecosystems).



- **[Enertiv](https://www.enertiv.com/)**  

  Real-time energy and equipment monitoring platform for commercial buildings and portfolios.



- **[Honeywell Forge](https://www.honeywell.com/)**  

  Enterprise performance and building platform that unifies operational data, analytics, and applications across building systems (often paired with Niagara).



- **[Johnson Controls OpenBlue](https://www.johnsoncontrols.com/)**  

  Smart building platform combining Metasys BMS heritage with cloud analytics, digital twins, and services for large facilities.



- **[Siemens Building X](https://www.siemens.com/)**  

  Cloud-native building software suite within the Siemens Xcelerator ecosystem for comfort, energy, safety, and operations.



- **[Schneider Electric EcoStruxure Building](https://www.se.com/)**  

  Open, IoT-enabled architecture for building management, energy, and integrated systems across commercial and critical facilities.



- **[Other BMS & intelligent building platforms](https://www.siemens.com/)**  

  Solutions from Trane, Carrier, ABB, Delta, and specialists covering controls, analytics, and multi-site operations.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[MyEMS](https://github.com/MyEMS/myems)**  

  Leading open-source Energy Management System aligned with ISO 50001. Supports collection, analysis, and reporting of electricity, water, gas, cooling, heating, and carbon data in buildings, with optional PV, storage, and microgrid capabilities.



- **[EMS-ESP / EMS-ESP32](https://github.com/emsesp/EMS-ESP32)**  

  Open-source firmware for ESP32 that reads and controls EMS/Heatronic-compatible boilers, thermostats, heat pumps, and related equipment—useful for building heating systems.



- **[BACnet and Modbus open-source stacks](https://github.com/search?q=BACnet+OR+bacnet-stack+OR+modbus)**  

  Libraries and gateways that enable communication with standard building automation protocols used by most commercial BMS.



- **[Home Assistant](https://github.com/home-assistant/core)**  

  Popular open-source home and building automation platform frequently extended for small commercial spaces, energy monitoring, and local control.



- **[ThingsBoard](https://github.com/thingsboard/thingsboard)**  

  Open-source IoT platform used for device management, dashboards, and rule engines in smart-building and energy projects.



- **[Node-RED + IoT flows](https://github.com/node-red/node-red)**  

  Flow-based programming tool widely used to integrate sensors, BMS data, and building automation logic.



- **[Open-source energy & carbon dashboards](https://github.com/search?q=building+energy+management+OR+smart+building+open+source)**  

  Community projects for visualization, sub-metering analytics, and basic fault detection.



- **[Tridium Niagara community & open tools](https://github.com/search?q=Niagara+Framework+OR+bacnet)**  

  Ecosystem tools and drivers around the widely deployed Niagara Framework (commercial core with open protocol support).



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **InfluxDB + Grafana**: Time-series storage and visualization stacks commonly used for building energy and sensor data.

- **MQTT brokers (Mosquitto, etc.)**: Messaging backbone for IoT sensors and edge devices in buildings.

- **OpenTherm / heating protocol tools**: Open-source interfaces for residential and light-commercial heating systems.

- **Digital twin & 3D viewers**: Lightweight open-source components for space and asset visualization.

- **Fault detection & diagnostics (FDD) research tools**: Academic and community code for HVAC analytics.

- CMMS and work-order systems that can be integrated with building data.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

For energy and sustainability monitoring, start with **MyEMS**.  

For local automation and integration, combine **Home Assistant**, **Node-RED**, **MQTT**, and BACnet/Modbus gateways.  

Enterprise multi-site BMS, life-safety integration, certified controls, and advanced AI optimization remain the domain of commercial platforms (Honeywell Forge, Johnson Controls OpenBlue, Siemens Building X, Schneider EcoStruxure, Facilio, Switch Automation, etc.).  

Many modern deployments use open-source layers for analytics and edge connectivity while retaining commercial BMS for core control and compliance.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Building automation systems affect comfort, safety, energy use, and in some cases life-safety systems. Any production deployment must respect local codes, cybersecurity best practices, and manufacturer guidelines.

- Open-source tools excel at monitoring, analytics, and integration but are rarely drop-in replacements for certified commercial BMS controllers and enterprise platforms used in large or regulated facilities.
